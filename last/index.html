If your score is 5 or higher, you are at high risk of developing prediabetes.

Read the quick facts below, then talk to your doctor about a simple blood test.

Prediabetes is common, and often silent

Most people with prediabetes do not know it. A simple check can catch it early.

NormalFasting blood sugar under 100 mg/dL (5.5 mmol/L)
Prediabetes100 to 125 mg/dL (5.6 to 6.9 mmol/L)
Diabetes126 mg/dL or more

Insulin is a key

Insulin lets sugar into your cells. When the lock sticks, sugar stays in your blood. This is called insulin resistance.

What raises your score?

  • Age 45 or older
  • Extra body weight
  • Family history of diabetes
  • Little exercise
  • High blood pressure
  • Diabetes during pregnancy

A blood test gives a clear answer

100 to 125 mg/dL (5.6 to 6.9 mmol/L) fasting blood sugar

5.7% to 6.4% A1C (3-month average)

140 to 199 mg/dL (7.8 to 11.0 mmol/L) 2-hour sugar test

Good news: you can lower your risk

  • Move 150 minutes a week
  • Eat more whole foods
  • Lose 5 to 7% of body weight if you carry extra
